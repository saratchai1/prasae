# Mangrove Monitoring Metrics

This dashboard intentionally separates **vegetation amount**, **vegetation condition**, **water/tide context**, and **observation quality**. It does not collapse them into a single uncalibrated health score.

## 1. Whole-plot NDVI

`NDVI = (B08 - B04) / (B08 + B04)`

- Sentinel-2 native bands: B08/B04 at 10 m.
- Reported as mean/median plus P10/P90 inside the exact plot polygon.
- Useful as plot-wide greenness context.
- Water and exposed substrate can strongly move the plot mean, so whole-plot NDVI change must be interpreted with Open Water and QA.

## 2. Green Cover Proxy

Green Cover is the percentage of valid in-boundary pixels whose NDVI exceeds the threshold assigned to that plot.

### Portfolio default

Plots without a site-specific reference use:

`NDVI > 0.25`

This is intentionally a conservative screening default.

### 13-STC / PLOT_076 calibration

PLOT_076 may use a site-specific threshold generated by `calibrate_green_proxy.py` and stored in:

`data/calibration/green_proxy_calibration.json`

The threshold is promoted only when the calibration passes an independent spatial holdout gate. If the gate fails, PLOT_076 automatically falls back to 0.25.

Current reference design:

- source: the georeferenced 13-STC drone tree-detection run
- dense positive reference: 10 m cells containing at least 10 HIGH-confidence detected trees
- strong negative tree-density reference: cells with zero accepted detections and nearest accepted detection at least 12 m away
- train/holdout separation: deterministic 50 m spatial blocks to reduce spatial leakage
- threshold selection: training blocks only
- holdout checks: precision, recall, balanced accuracy, and material improvement over the 0.25 baseline

The negative class means **low detected tree density**, not guaranteed absence of every kind of vegetation. For that reason this calibration is explicitly a Green Cover proxy alignment, not a tree/no-tree classifier.

Important:

- Green Cover is a **proxy**, not field canopy cover, survival rate, tree count, or carbon stock.
- Drone detector output is not treated as field ground truth.
- The site-specific threshold must not be copied to unrelated plots.
- Every observation records `green_proxy_threshold`, `green_proxy_calibration_status`, and `green_proxy_calibration_source`.
- MFI is deliberately **not** merged into Green Cover.

## 3. Vegetation-only NDVI

Median NDVI calculated only over pixels passing the Green Cover Proxy.

This separates two questions:

- **How much visible green vegetation is present?** -> Green Cover Proxy
- **How green is the detected vegetation?** -> Vegetation-only NDVI

## 4. NDRE red-edge diagnostic

`NDRE = (B8A - B05) / (B8A + B05)`

Calculated over Green Cover pixels. B8A/B05 are 20 m Sentinel-2 bands and are resampled to the common target grid for aligned plot statistics.

NDRE is retained as a vegetation-condition diagnostic; it is not converted into biomass or carbon without field calibration.

## 5. Open Water

Current screening rule:

`MNDWI = (B03 - B11) / (B03 + B11)`

`Open Water = MNDWI > 0 AND NOT Green Cover`

Open Water is shown alongside Green Cover because tidal/water conditions can alter apparent vegetation metrics in mangrove restoration areas.

## 6. MFI signal in water

The Sentinel-2 Mangrove Forest Index (MFI; Jia et al., 2019) is calculated from red, red-edge and SWIR2 reflectance.

The dashboard reports:

`MFI signal in water = MFI > 0 AND Open Water`

This is a **diagnostic of possible submerged mangrove spectral signal**. It is not added to Green Cover and must not be described as measured canopy area.

## 7. Observation QA and comparability

Every date records:

- clear-pixel percentage inside the plot
- number of Sentinel-2 scenes used
- source scene IDs and timestamps
- processing status
- Open Water percentage
- Green Cover threshold and calibration provenance

Dashboard context labels:

- `LOW QA`: clear pixels < 30%
- `TIDE-DOMINATED`: Open Water > 40%
- `WATER-INFLUENCED`: Open Water > 20%
- `COMPARABLE`: otherwise

These labels are screening aids, not statistical confidence intervals.

## 8. No fabricated time series

The system has exactly 12 declared observation/composite months. It does not:

- interpolate missing months
- substitute the nearest available image while displaying another date
- generate synthetic NDVI values

Missing or poor observations remain `no_data` / `insufficient_clear_pixels`.

## 9. Calibration boundary and next evidence level

The current 13-STC calibration is stronger than an arbitrary threshold because it is aligned to independent geospatial drone evidence and checked on spatially held-out cells. It is still not equivalent to field ground truth.

The next evidence level is to add human/field-reviewed polygon or pixel labels for representative classes:

- mangrove canopy / seedlings
- grass or other non-mangrove vegetation
- open water
- mud / exposed substrate

Those labels should be kept spatially separate between training and validation. Once available, the same calibration framework can compare a threshold model against a supervised Sentinel-2 classifier without changing historical observations or fabricating missing data.
